How to play

Answer right, then poison the bottles.

  • 10 Halloween questions, each with three possible answers.
  • Answer correctly and you unlock the poison bonus: guess the order of four bottles. The real order is hidden, and it changes on every question.
  • You get three attempts to match it exactly. Match on the 1st attempt and the question is worth 5 points, on the 2nd 3 points, on the 3rd 2 points. Run out of attempts and you still keep 1 point. Answer wrong and it is worth 0.
  • Your points and the question counter stay at the top of the page throughout.
  • The final screen shows your total points, the time you took and when you finished.
